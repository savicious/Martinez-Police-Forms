# Privacy Policy — Martinez Police Forms

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Developer:** Code 3 Coders LLC  
**Platform:** Apple iOS

## 1. Overview

Martinez Police Forms is an iOS application developed by Code 3 Coders LLC to assist authorized law enforcement personnel with completing departmental forms, capturing electronic signatures, and generating PDF documents.

The application operates entirely on the user's iPhone. All information entered into the application is processed and stored locally on the device.

**Code 3 Coders LLC does not collect, access, transmit, sell, or share information entered into the application.**

## 2. Information Processed

The application may process the following information:

- Officer names and agency information
- Incident and case numbers
- Names and dates of birth
- Business and property addresses
- Electronic signatures
- Form details and authorization acknowledgments

This information is used exclusively to complete departmental forms and generate documents.

## 3. Local Data Storage

All application data is stored exclusively on the user's iPhone.

Martinez Police Forms does not:

- Upload information to cloud storage or external servers
- Maintain external databases
- Require user accounts or registration
- Collect analytics or usage statistics
- Use advertising or tracking technologies
- Transmit application data to Code 3 Coders LLC or third parties

Code 3 Coders LLC cannot remotely access, retrieve, or modify information stored within the application.

## 4. Electronic Signatures

Electronic signatures are captured and processed locally on the iPhone.

Signatures are incorporated into generated documents and are never uploaded to external servers by the application.

## 5. PDF Generation and Sharing

PDF documents are generated locally on the iPhone.

Users may manually export, print, or share completed documents using available iOS functionality.

The application does not automatically transmit or distribute documents.

Once exported, documents are subject to the storage and security practices of the destination selected by the user.

## 6. Data Security

Users are responsible for protecting information stored on their devices.

We recommend maintaining device passcode protection, keeping iOS updated, and following applicable departmental information-security policies.

Code 3 Coders LLC does not maintain copies of user-generated documents or application data.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Application information remains locally stored on the iPhone until deleted by the user or removed through applicable iOS data-management processes.

Deleting the application generally removes its locally stored application data. Copies retained in device backups or previously exported documents may remain separately.

Code 3 Coders LLC cannot remotely recover deleted application information.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Martinez Police Forms does not integrate external analytics, advertising, cloud storage, or third-party data-processing services.

Any sharing initiated by the user through iOS is controlled by the user and the selected destination.

## 9. Law Enforcement Information

The application is intended for authorized law enforcement use.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information is handled according to applicable laws, regulations, and departmental policies.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Martinez Police Forms is not directed toward children and does not independently collect personal information from children.

Authorized personnel may enter information concerning minors when necessary for lawful departmental documentation.

## 11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or applicable requirements.

Updates will be published with a revised effective date.
